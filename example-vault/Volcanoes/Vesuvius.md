# Vesuvius

The [[Stratovolcano]] above the Bay of Naples, Italy. In AD 79 it buried the Roman towns of
Pompeii and Herculaneum under ash and [[Pyroclastic Flow|pyroclastic flows]]. The eruption was
described by [[Pliny the Younger]], which is why tall, explosive eruptions are called *Plinian*.
It last erupted in 1944.
