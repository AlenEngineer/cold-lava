# Mount St. Helens

A [[Stratovolcano]] in the Cascade Range of Washington State, on the [[Ring of Fire]].

On 18 May 1980 its north side collapsed in a huge landslide, unleashing a sideways blast. It was
a VEI 5 eruption (see [[Volcanic Explosivity Index]]) and killed 57 people, including the
volcanologist [[David A. Johnston]]. Mudflows like a [[Lahar]] rushed down the river valleys.
