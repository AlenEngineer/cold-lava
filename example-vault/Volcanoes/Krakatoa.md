# Krakatoa

A volcanic island in Indonesia, between Java and Sumatra, on the [[Ring of Fire]]. Its 1883
eruption (VEI 6, see [[Volcanic Explosivity Index]]) was heard thousands of kilometres away, and
its tsunamis killed more than 36,000 people. A new island, Anak Krakatau ("child of Krakatoa"),
rose from the sea in 1927.
