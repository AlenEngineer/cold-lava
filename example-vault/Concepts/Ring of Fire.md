# Ring of Fire

A horseshoe of [[Subduction Zone|subduction zones]] around the Pacific Ocean, about 40,000 km
long. Most of the world's active volcanoes are on it, including [[Mount St. Helens]],
[[Mount Fuji]] and [[Krakatoa]]. It's the classic example of [[Plate Tectonics]] at work.
