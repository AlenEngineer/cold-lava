# Lava

Molten rock that reaches the surface. Below ground it's called magma.

## Temperature
Basaltic lava, the runny kind at a [[Shield Volcano]], erupts at roughly 1,100–1,200 °C.

## Textures
When it cools, basaltic lava usually becomes either smooth, ropy [[Pāhoehoe]] or rough, jagged
[[ʻAʻā]]. Both names come from Hawaiian.
