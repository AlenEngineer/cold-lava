# Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI)

A scale from 0 to 8 for how big an explosive eruption is, based mostly on the volume of material
thrown out. Each step is about ten times bigger than the last.

| VEI | Example |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | [[Eyjafjallajökull]] 2010 |
| 5 | [[Mount St. Helens]] 1980, [[Vesuvius]] AD 79 |
| 6 | [[Krakatoa]] 1883 |
| 8 | [[Yellowstone]] (see [[Supervolcano]]) |
