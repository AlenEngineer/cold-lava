# Stratovolcano

Steep cones built from alternating layers (strata) of lava, ash and rock. Their sticky magma traps
gas, so they tend to erupt explosively, sometimes with a deadly [[Pyroclastic Flow]]. They form
above a [[Subduction Zone]]. Examples: [[Mount Fuji]], [[Vesuvius]] and [[Mount St. Helens]].
