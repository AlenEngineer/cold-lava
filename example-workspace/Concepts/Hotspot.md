# Hotspot

A plume of unusually hot mantle that melts through the plate above it. As the plate drifts, it
leaves a trail of volcanoes, like the Hawaiian islands with [[Kīlauea]] and [[Mauna Loa]] at the
young end. [[Yellowstone]] sits over a continental hotspot. Part of [[Plate Tectonics]].
