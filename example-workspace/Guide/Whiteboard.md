# Whiteboard

Press `w` in the graph, or run `cold-lava board example-workspace`.

Boards are Obsidian Canvas files (`.canvas`), so they open in Obsidian too. This workspace has three:

- **Welcome**: a guided tour with a checklist of things to try.
- **Eruptions Timeline**: note cards for [[Vesuvius]], [[Krakatoa]], [[Mount St. Helens]] and
  [[Eyjafjallajökull]], connected in order.
- **Volcano Venn**: placed shapes. A Venn of the volcanoes that erupted and the
  stratovolcanoes, which shows what each region holds as the notes change; a card on
  Krakatoa; a sticky with a task; an arrow; and a checklist of the field season's tasks, where
  a click ticks one. `s` places another shape, `x` shows what a shape can do (the sticky can
  become a note), and `r` or the `↻` grip turns one.

![[Volcano Venn.canvas]]

If you have a board open in both apps, edits from either side are merged, not overwritten.
