# Kīlauea

One of the most active volcanoes on Earth, on the Island of Hawaiʻi. It's a [[Shield Volcano]] fed
by the Hawaiian [[Hotspot]], and its lava flows are mostly [[Pāhoehoe]] and [[ʻAʻā]].

In 2018 an eruption on its lower flank destroyed around 700 homes.

Neighbour: [[Mauna Loa]].
