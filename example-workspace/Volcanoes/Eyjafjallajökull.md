# Eyjafjallajökull

An ice-capped volcano in [[Iceland]]. Its fairly small 2010 eruption (VEI 4, see
[[Volcanic Explosivity Index]]) sent an ash cloud over Europe that shut down air travel for days,
the biggest air-traffic shutdown since World War II.
